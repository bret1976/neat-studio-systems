NEAT company concept
NEAT is a proposed architectural intelligence platform that brings model analysis, design coordination, rule-based review, AI-assisted recommendations, and controlled automation into the architectural design process.
The goal is to reduce repetitive coordination work while helping architects and technical design teams focus on higher-value design decisions.
NEAT is conceived around two complementary products:
NEAT Assist
An intelligent assistant for reviewing and interacting with architectural models.
NEAT Control
A centralized workspace for model issues, team reviews, approvals, project standards, and design quality oversight.
Both products should be presented as part of a coherent architecture workflow ecosystem.
Until actual integrations and production services are completed, product functionality must be clearly distinguished from interactive marketing demonstrations.
